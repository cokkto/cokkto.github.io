Cartography of Weights and Scales by Aleksi Salo · Dream rock / Art pop · 2026

      Cartography of Weights and Scales is an album about inner geography measured through small matters. Its songs move between dream rock, art rock, dark pop, trip hop, early rhythm-and-blues shadows, and stripped post-rock textures, keeping the voice low, close, and controlled while the arrangements drift through guitars, bass pulse, piano fragments, tape-worn ambience, and restrained drums.

      The album avoids direct confession. Instead, the psyche appears through surfaces and rituals: a mirror too high to reach, sand in the mouth of morning duties, creeks speaking underfoot, a hidden shelder hiding its address, a motor plane marked by a Red Devil, and a final inventory of things too small to save but too intimate to abandon. The songs are not about escape as freedom, but about the strange moment when escape itself becomes a coordinate.

      By the end, Cartography of Weights and Scales turns into a private map of wrongness, weight, and belonging. Society is not fought directly; it is outlived, misread, slipped from, measured against. The album closes not with triumph, but with selection: what still answers, what still has warmth, what can be carried when the world no longer recognizes the address.
